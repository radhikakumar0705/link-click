# link-click privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

link-click is a browser extension that shortens URLs. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What the extension collects

- **URLs you choose to shorten.** When you click **Shorten** or use the right-click menu, the extension sends that one URL to the link-click server. It doesn't read the other pages you visit, your browsing history, or page content.
- **Account details, if you sign in:** your email address, an optional display name, and your password. The password goes to the server only over HTTPS when you sign in or register, and is stored there only as a hash.
- **On this device** (`chrome.storage.local`): your sign-in tokens, your settings, and your 50 most recent short links. Signing out deletes the tokens and history.

## What the link-click server records

- The short links you create, together with your account if you're signed in.
- When someone opens a short link: the time, country and city (looked up from the IP address), device type, browser, operating system, and referring site. **IP addresses are never stored.** They're hashed with a salt that rotates daily, and the hash is used only to count unique visitors.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share your data, or use it for advertising.
- We don't track your browsing.
- URLs may be checked against Google Safe Browsing to block malicious links. That check sends the URL only.

## Your choices

- You can use the extension without an account.
- You can delete any link from the History tab.
- To delete your account and data, contact the address below. Your links are anonymised and your click data is deleted within 24 hours.
